Emergency Basic Life-Saving Training

The Emergency Basic Life-Saving Training aims to equip young people with essential knowledge and practical skills in responding to emergencies, providing basic first aid, and potentially saving lives while waiting for professional responders. The training is open to youth and interested participants in the community, especially those who want to develop emergency preparedness and life-saving skills as part of Linggo ng Kabataan (LNK).
